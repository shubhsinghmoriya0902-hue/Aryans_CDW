# Calibration Against Placed Resumes

This document records how the scoring engine's weights, thresholds, and
sub-score curves were tuned against a corpus of **real IIT Kanpur resumes
belonging to students who were actually placed**, and what was learned in the
process. It exists so the scoring is defensible as empirically grounded rather
than hand-waved — and so the ATR can cite a concrete methodology.

## Corpus

| Track | Resumes analyzed |
|---|---|
| SDE | 36 |
| Quant Finance | 21 |
| Management Consulting | 11 |
| Core Engineering | 6 |
| **Total** | **74** |

All were PDFs of placed candidates, grouped by the track they placed into.
Small-N tracks (Core especially) are treated as *indicative*, not definitive.

## Important methodological caveat

This corpus contains only **placed** resumes — there are no rejected resumes
to contrast against. That means the calibration answers *"what does a placed
resume in track X look like?"* and tunes the score so placed candidates land
in a sensible range and score highest on their own track. It does **not**
establish predictive validity (placed-vs-unplaced discrimination), which would
require a labeled negative set. This limitation is stated plainly in the ATR.

## What the placed data showed

Signals extracted per track (medians / rates across the corpus):

| Signal | SDE | Quant | Consulting | Core |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CPI median | 8.1 | 8.8 | 8.3 | 8.35 |
| CPI p25 | 7.77 | 8.68 | 7.56 | 8.20 |
| CPI min | 7.1 | 7.5 | 6.8 | 8.2 |
| Has GitHub link | 94% | 100% | 64% | 67% |
| Has PoR section | 36% | 67% | 82% | 33% |
| Has Coursework section | 69% | 67% | 18% | 83% |
| Impact-metric density (median) | 0.07 | 0.11 | 0.15 | 0.03 |
| Strong-verb fraction (median) | 0.23 | 0.36 | 0.11 | 0.20 |
| Keyword-pool coverage (p75) | 0.21 | 0.15 | 0.29 | 0.08 |

### Key findings that changed the engine

1. **Even placed candidates quantify only a minority of bullets.** Impact-
   metric density ran ~0.07–0.15 by track, not the ~0.5 the original scoring
   implicitly rewarded. Ranks, years, and course names contain digits but
   aren't impact metrics. *Fix:* impact-density now hits full marks at ~0.22
   density (≈ placed p75) instead of demanding near-total quantification.

2. **Nobody lists half the keyword pool.** Placed resumes matched a median of
   ~0.11 (SDE) to ~0.22 (Consulting) of a track's weighted keyword pool. The
   original flat 0.55 coverage target made the projects/coursework components
   structurally unreachable (placed SDE median projects sub-score was 10/100).
   *Fix:* per-track `keyword_target` / `coursework_target` set near each
   track's placed p75.

3. **PoR titles live in header lines, not description bullets.** The matcher
   was fed bullet descriptions ("Organized...", "Led...") instead of the role
   titles ("Secretary, Debating Society"), scoring 82%-PoR-holding Consulting
   candidates at 0/100 on leadership. *Fix:* the matcher now reads the PoR
   section's non-bullet title lines, strips trailing dates / `| body`
   metadata, and matches those.

4. **CPI selectivity is track-specific.** Quant placed CPIs cluster tightly
   high (p25 8.68); Consulting spreads wide (min 6.8), confirming the PS's
   "spikes over CPI" framing. *Fix:* per-track CPI `good`/`excellent`
   thresholds anchored to each track's placed distribution (Quant highest).

5. **Noun-led bullets are normal, not weak.** Many placed bullets open with a
   project/company noun rather than a verb. *Fix:* the action-verb sub-score
   treats an 'unknown' opener as neutral (0.55), reserving the 0 floor for
   genuinely passive phrasing ("Worked on", "Responsible for").

6. **Two over-aggressive penalties were firing on placed candidates.**
   `generic_project_no_impact` hit 28% of placed SDE resumes (it looked at the
   Projects section in isolation); `no_leadership_por` hit 100% of placed
   Consulting (it required Mid+ tier). *Fix:* the impact penalty now considers
   the whole narrative body; the PoR penalty fires only when there is no
   catalogue match at all.

### Parser bugs surfaced by the real corpus (and fixed)

- **List-line pollution:** Skills/Coursework lines ("Programming Languages: C,
  C++, Python", "Linear Algebra, Calculus-I") were counted as achievement
  bullets, dragging impact/verb stats toward zero. Impact analysis now runs
  only on narrative sections (Experience, Projects, PoR, Achievements, etc.).
- **`cad` false positive:** the bare substring "cad" matched inside
  "aCADemic", making every resume look CAD-proficient. Short alphanumeric
  keywords are now matched on word boundaries.
- **Page-footer sections:** running headers like "Shrey Mehta · Curriculum
  Vitae 1" were parsed as section headings. Footer/date-bearing lines are now
  excluded from heading detection.

## Resulting behavior

After calibration, on their own track the placed corpus scores (median):
SDE ≈ 47, Quant ≈ 58, Consulting ≈ 47, Core ≈ 19 (Core low largely because
those 6 resumes carry web/ML projects with sparse core-domain keywords — a
real signal, and a small sample). Crucially, **cross-track discrimination
holds**: SDE and Consulting resumes score highest on their own track, and the
same resume produces materially different scores across tracks — the core
"Role-Targeted analysis" requirement of the problem statement.

Scores are intentionally *not* inflated into the 80s+ for every placed
candidate: doing so would flatten the tool's ability to discriminate. The
score is a diagnostic dial, not a pass/fail stamp.

## Reproducing the calibration

The per-track statistics above were produced by running every resume through
`engine.parse_resume` + `engine.score_resume` and aggregating the component
sub-scores and extracted signals. The tuned constants live in
`engine/data/role_baselines.py` (thresholds, weights, coverage targets) and
`engine/scoring.py` (sub-score curves). `tests/test_engine.py::TestCalibration`
locks in the key calibrated behaviors so future edits can't silently regress
them.
