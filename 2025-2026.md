Based on parsing and analyzing **88 official SPO LaTeX PDF resumes** across the 4 major placement tracks (*Software Engineering*, *Quantitative Finance*, *Management Consulting & Analytics*, and *Core Engineering*), here is the detailed empirical breakdown of what differentiates successful profiles in each domain.

## 1.  Quantitative Finance & Trading (N=17)

### Key Observations:
1. **CPI Sensitivity is Paramount**:
   * Median CPI is **8.80**, with a tight interquartile range (8.40 – 9.20).
   * Over **76%** of candidates had a CPI $\ge 8.50$.
   * Even the single outlier candidate with $<7.0$ CPI compensated with exceptional CP ratings (Candidate Master on Codeforces) and international algorithmic achievements.

2. **Core Technical Stack & Tools**:
   * **Languages**: Python (NumPy, Pandas, SciPy, Matplotlib), C++ (STL, template metaprogramming, low-latency structures).
   * **Math & Stat Keywords**: Linear Algebra, Probability & Random Processes, Stochastic Calculus, Optimization, Monte Carlo Simulations, Time Series Analysis (ARIMA, GARCH), Black-Scholes Formula.

3. **Competitive Programming & IITK Jargon**:
   * **64.7%** explicitly list **Codeforces** / **CodeChef** ratings.
   * Prominent IITK entities: `CPI`, `Codeforces`, `Inter IIT Tech`, `SURGE`, `CodeChef`.

4. **Positions of Responsibility (PoRs)**:
   * Strong presence of academic/mentorship PoRs: *Academic Mentor (AnC Council)*, *Student Guide (Counselling Service)*, *Secretary (Science & Tech / MnP Club)*.

---

## 2.  Software Engineering (SDE & AI/ML/Systems) (N=42)

### Key Observations:
1. **CPI Distribution**:
   * Median CPI is **8.30** (25th percentile: 7.65, 75th percentile: 9.11).
   * Wider CPI variance than Quant; candidates with CPI 7.0–7.8 regularly cleared top product firms (Uber, Atlassian, Rippling, Intuit) if they had strong systems projects, Open Source / GSoC, or high CF ratings.

2. **Core Technical Stack & Tools**:
   * **Languages**: C++, Java, Python, Go, TypeScript/JavaScript.
   * **Frameworks & Infrastructure**: React, Node.js, Express, FastAPI, Django, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Microservices, GraphQL.
   * **Systems & Core CS**: DSA, Operating Systems, Computer Networks, DBMS, System Design, Concurrency, Multithreading.

3. **Project & Verification Standards**:
   * **90.5%** included verifiable clickable GitHub repository / demo links.
   * High density of real-world deployed projects with user metrics (e.g. *"Processed 10k+ requests/sec with <50ms latency"*, *"Reduced query execution time by 40% using Redis caching"*).

4. **IITK Jargon & Extracurriculars**:
   * Entities: `Codeforces` (38%), `Inter IIT Tech Contingent`, `Antaragni`, `Kaggle`, `AnC Council`.
   * PoRs: *Web Coordinator*, *Secretary (Programming Club / Web Division)*, *Company Coordinator (SPO)*.

## 3.  Management Consulting & Business Analytics (N=19)

### Key Observations:
1. **The "Spike" Profile**:
   * High CPI (Median **8.70**, Min **8.00** — remarkably high academic floor at IITK for top consulting shortlists like McKinsey, Nomura Strategy).
   * High-tier PoR ownership (**Tier 1 & Tier 2 leadership**).
   * Cultural/Sports achievements or National Case Competitions.

2. **Highest Quantified Impact Density (16.5%)**:
   * Consulting resumes boast the highest proportion of quantified bullet points (**16.5% average**, P75 reaching **21.4%**).
   * Metrics focus heavily on business ROI: revenue increase (`₹/$/%`), cost reduction, turnaround time, market reach, user adoption.

3. **Key Vocab & Coursework**:
   * Vocab: GTM Strategy, Market Sizing, Unit Economics, Cost Optimization, EBITDA, Stakeholder Management, Financial Modeling, PowerBI, Advanced Excel.
   * Coursework: Economics (ECO101/ECO201), Principles of Management (MBA600-series), Game Theory, Operations Research, Industrial Engineering.

4. **Top Leadership PoRs**:
   * *Senate Representative / Senator*, *Parliamentarian*, *Manager / Core Team (Antaragni / Techkriti)*, *Academic Mentor*, *Company Coordinator*.

---

## 4.  Core Engineering (Mech, EE, Civil, Chem, Hardware) (N=10)

### Key Observations:
1. **CPI Distribution**:
   * Median CPI is **8.15** (25th percentile: 7.55, 75th percentile: 8.55).

2. **Domain-Specific Software Tools**:
   * **Mechanical/Aero/Civil**: SolidWorks, AutoCAD, ANSYS Fluent / Mechanical, FEA, CFD, MATLAB / Simulink, Abaqus, OpenFOAM.
   * **Electrical/Hardware (TI, Qualcomm, Google Silicon)**: Verilog, VHDL, Cadence Virtuoso, LTSpice, Digital/Analog IC Design, FPGA.

3. **Research & Practical Core Projects**:
   * Heavy weight on **SURGE internships**, departmental research under faculty, institute tech teams (*AUV-IITK*, *Motorsports / SAE*, *Aerial Robotics*), and published conference papers / patents.
   * Bullets are technically dense and emphasize physical prototyping, simulation validation, and fabrication tolerances.

##  Engine Parameter Calibrations

Based on these empirical distributions, the diagnostic engine's role baselines have been calibrated:

1. **Quant Finance**:
   * Calibrated CPI thresholds: `good = 8.4`, `excellent = 9.2` (reflecting the 8.40–9.20 IQR).
   * Enhanced weighting for Linear Algebra, Stochastic Calculus, Probability, and algorithmic proficiency signals.

2. **Software Engineering (SDE)**:
   * Calibrated CPI thresholds: `good = 7.7`, `excellent = 8.8` (reflecting median 8.30 and p25 7.65).
   * Target coverage for keyword and project depth aligned with realistic multi-stack profiles.

3. **Management Consulting**:
   * Calibrated CPI thresholds: `good = 8.2`, `excellent = 8.9` (reflecting the empirical 8.00 minimum floor in placed shortlists).
   * Impact density target calibrated to reward high-performing resumes with $\ge 15\%$ quantified statements.
   * Enhanced PoR multiplier to reward Tier 1/2 spikes.

4. **Core Engineering**:
   * Calibrated CPI thresholds: `good = 7.5`, `excellent = 8.6`.
   * Added explicit recognition for CAD/FEA/CFD/VLSI toolchains and campus tech teams (`AUV-IITK`, `Motorsports`).
